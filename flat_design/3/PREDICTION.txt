THE SESSION'S RESTATEMENT (the session's words)
  Both sides in up/hole form: every place of every light value and every weight is a photon up or a hole. At one place
  pair (light place a, plate place b) of one gate (neuron) the N lines meet; agreeing meetings and disagreeing meetings
  pair off (the flat), and what is left is the tip (the extra). The pile is the tips alone: sum of 2^(a+b) x tip.
  The angle given to this designer: count the ups and downs of light and plate together as classes, so that no pair
  that cancels is ever formed one by one.

ANALYSIS BEFORE THE RUN (the session's)
  1. Three digit forms give three different flats and three different extras:
     up/hole both sides: N meetings at every one of 16 x 16 place pairs, 256 a weight; the tip is a coin toss over N.
     1s both sides (plain): only lit x lit pixels meet, about 32 a weight on GPT-2.
     signed digits both sides: about 58.7% of plain meet.
     The "about 3%" is the up/hole form's extra as a share of the up/hole meetings (256 a weight). In plain's own
     photons that is 256 x 0.0288 = 7.4 a weight against plain's ~32: about 23% at 768-line plates, and
     256 x 0.0144 = 3.7, about 11.5%, at mproj's 3,072 lines. So the up/hole extra, photon by photon, is roughly
     where the group machine already stands. The group machine's reads (about 18% of plain at the plates) are about
     18% x 32 / 256 = 2.3% of the up/hole meetings: already below the 2.9% extra in that unit.
  2. A class count (popcount of agreeing lines) cannot be made without reading every pixel of both planes, so a
     bit-plane machine pays at least 256 pixels a weight in photons (the 14B class-count reading cost, 313 a weight,
     is this) and about 8 word operations a weight; it cannot win in any of the three units.
  3. Group piles are the class count with the cancelling done inside a whole number: a ready pile of g lines is
     sum over light places of 2^a x (agree - disagree) at one plate pattern, written as one number of about 4.5
     signed digits. A ready pile serves only the gates whose plate shows the same pattern of g pixels. On a plate
     where every pattern is about equally likely, a table of 2^g piles pays only while 2^g is not far above the
     number of readers (gates x up/hole places that read one light). This fixes the work a weight from below:
        reads a weight >= (up/hole places a weight) / g,   g <= about log2(readers),
        work a weight  >= (places / log2(readers)) x (signed digits of a ready pile) + table.
     GPT-2 readers: 14 x 768 = 2^13.4 at proj and mproj, 14 x 2,304 = 2^15.0 at qkv, 14 x 3,072 = 2^15.4 at fc.
     The floor is about 15-17% of plain at the plates. Approaching 3% of plain would need a ready pile that covers
     about 80 lines, a table of 2^80. No exact class machine reaches it on GPT-2's random plates.

THE ONE METHOD PROPOSED (the session's): TWO-SIDED PER-PLACE GROUP PILES WITH THE READERS RELATION
  - Each side split by place, each split fixed by that side alone:
      plate: dense places up/hole (addresses), sparse top places in signed digits met directly (the confirmed rule,
             L_b < G x M), as in base2_groups.py;
      light: dense places inside the ready piles (whole numbers: the light's own ups and downs cancel there), sparse
             top places (the few large lines' top signed digits, the mirror rule: fewer lit digits than groups,
             L^x_a < G, read from the light alone each position) met directly against each gate's whole weight.
  - g at each plate from the readers relation, not searched: the g that solves
        2^(g-1) x (g ln2 - 1) = R x s_V / (s_x + o_V)
    R = up/hole places x gates (readers of one table), s_V the ready piles' mean signed digits, s_x the light's mean
    signed digits (each table step), o_V the ready piles' mean 1s (their writing); taken from the g = 12 sample run,
    rounded to the nearest whole g.
  - Exact by linearity: 2 x.w = sum over up/hole places of 2^b x ready pile(x_lo) + (2^h - 1) X_lo
                                + 2^(h+1) x_lo . w_top + 2 x_top . w.
