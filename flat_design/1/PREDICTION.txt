DESIGNER 1 (plate side first). Written 2026-10-08, before sizes.py runs. The session's prediction, not his.

THE RELATION (from the plate alone and the light's measured digits; no sweep, no winner picked by result)
  Work a weight at one plate, in photons (every add counted, table and writing included):
      W(g) = (S / g) * dT  +  2^(g-1) * (dstep + dwrite) / (M * g)  +  sd reads  +  X
  S      up/hole sheets a weight's group reads: 14.3 (1.19 reads a weight at g = 12, groups2 compare.json)
  dT     photons a ready packet: 4.65 (qkv, fc, mproj), 5.03 (proj)      (compare.json read / read_n)
  dstep  photons a table step: the light's signed digits, 3.9-4.2
  dwrite photons to write a read packet in signed digits: its 1s, about 6.5
  M      gates (neurons) that read one light: 2,304 qkv, 768 proj, 3,072 fc, 768 mproj
  Going from g to g+1 pays only while  M*S*dT*(1/g - 1/(g+1))  >  (2^g/(g+1) - 2^(g-1)/g) * (dstep + dwrite).
  That gives g = 11 at the 768-gate plates (proj, mproj) and g = 12 at qkv and fc (13 does not pay there).

PREDICTION FOR THE SAMPLE (layer 5, the 48 positions of excess_sample.py, every pile checked against x @ w)

WHAT THIS CANNOT DO (the relation's own limit, written before any run)
  The reads already sit at the plate's information: 14.3 sheets a weight against the stored floor of 14.4 bits a
  weight. A read carries one g-bit address. So no exact machine that adds one ready packet a sheet can go below
  about (14.4 / g) x 4.65 photons a weight, about 17% of plain at GPT-2's widths, whatever the plate is rewritten as.
