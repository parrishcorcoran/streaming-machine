THE SESSION'S RESTATEMENT (not his words)
  In the up/hole form every place of the light and of the plate is lit: a photon up or a hole. Every meeting is an up
  or a down, and nearly all of them pair off: that pairing is the flat. What is left at each place pair is the tip,
  the extra photons above the flat. The tips alone rebuild every pile. His machine: skip the flat on both sides, and
  add only the extra photons, at every gate they are in.

WHAT IS MEASURED HERE (GPT-2, base2's own light and plates, a sample: layer 5's qkv, proj, fc, mproj, 24 positions)
  E1  up/hole place-pair tips: sum over the 16 x 16 place pairs of |N - 2d| (FINDINGS sec. 5's definition)
  E2  the same in the lit form (sign and 1s: only lit places meet; same signs up, opposite down)
  E3  the lit tips paired along each diagonal (a + b = d) before taking the size
  E4  the gate's place sums (each plate place b: the whole light against that place), counted in signed digits
  E5  the pile's own signed digits
  each as a share of the up/hole meetings (N x 256) and of plain (lit light x lit plate, binary 1s)

PREDICTIONS
  P1  E1 / up/hole meetings = the coin toss: 2.88% at the 768-line plates, 1.44% at mproj (3,072 lines).
  P2  THE UNIT. Up/hole meets every place pair, plain only the lit ones: about 256 / (5.1 x 6.3) = 8x more. So E1 as a
      share of PLAIN is about 2.88% x 8 = 22-24% at qkv, proj, fc, and about 11% at mproj. The group machine at
      layer 5 (20.5%, 26.6%, 20.6%, 25.5% of plain) is therefore already AT the place-pair excess at the 768-line
      plates. The "3%" is 3% of the up/hole meetings, not of plain.
  P3  E2 / plain: 6-10% (768 lines), 3-6% (mproj).
  P4  E3 / plain: 1.5-3% (768), 0.8-1.5% (mproj).
  P5  E4 / plain: 0.3-0.8%.   E5 / plain: 0.02-0.06%.
  P6  The excess has no floor of its own: the wider the pairing, the smaller it gets, down to the pile's own photons.
      The work is set by something else (P8).
  P7  CONTROLS. Shuffle (each position's light across its lines): E1-E5 move under 0.3 points of plain (these are
      coin-toss quantities; rule 13.3 says they must return chance, and the point of measuring them is the unit, not
      structure). Order-free (lines reordered the same way on both sides): every number identical. Exact: both tip
      forms rebuild every pile (0 differ). An independent counter (packed popcounts) gives the same E1 to the photon.
  P8  THE FLOOR, the session's relation: one ready pile, read by one gate at one place, names one of 2^g patterns, so it
      carries at most g bits of the plate; the plate holds about 12.5 bits a weight at the places read; so a gate
      needs at least about 12.5 / g reads per weight it holds, and each read adds the ready pile's own photons
      (its signed digits, about 4.3: a group's light). With g capped near 12 (the table is rebuilt every token and
      shared only by the gate-places reading that light), reads alone are about 1.05 x 4.3 / 32 = 14% of plain, and
      the table and writing add 1.5-4 points per plate. The group machine sits on this relation. 3% would need one
      read to carry about 50 plate bits, or a ready pile of about 1 photon. Neither is open.

THE ONE METHOD (the session's name: THE TIPS MACHINE AT ITS FLOOR)
  His picture followed forward is the group machine, read from the tips:
   - the light's flat cancels once a token inside each ready pile (a random group's ups and holes against each other),
     shared by every gate;
   - the plate's flat (every hole) is skipped by the flip: a pattern's complement is the opposite ready pile;
   - skipping the flat on BOTH sides leaves one cross term, one number a token (X, the light summed) and one a gate
     (kcorr): that is why it has to be both sides; it costs about 0.05% of plain;
   - each gate, at each up/hole place, adds the one ready pile its pattern names: the extra photons, added per gate.
  The one change the tips view adds: the table width at each plate is set by the floor relation, not fixed at 12.
  Build + writing per group a token, 2^(g-1) x (c_build + c_write), is balanced against the reads it removes,
  users x c_read / g, users = gates x up/hole places. Fixed from the plate alone, before any light:
    qkv (2,304 gates), fc (3,072 gates): g = 12 (unchanged)
    proj, mproj (768 gates): g = 11
  Sample prediction (layer 5, every pile identical): proj 26.6% -> 25.0%, mproj 25.5% -> 23.6% of plain; qkv, fc
  unchanged.
  Whole GPT-2: 711.4M -> about 693M a token, 19.40% -> 18.9% (18.7-19.1%).
  Whole GPT-2 at the floor (every plate's reads at 12.5/g reads a weight, the table at its balance): 16-17%.
  This does NOT approach 3%, and P8 says no exact machine of this kind can.
