ONLY THE EXTRA -- prediction, written 2026-10-08 before onlyextra.py ran.

His words (2026-10-08): "TERNARY and 4 bit quantization proves it's not necessary to store all the extras."
"Ternary is 10x smaller and is the worst way to get rid of the extra. We already proved the extra is all we need"

The picture (the session's restatement): every weight and every light value is a stack of photons on circles of 2.
The low photons are speckle that cancels in every pile (the flat is the cancelling). The extra stands above the flat
in the top corner, where the light's top photons meet the plate's top photons. So keep only each weight's top k
photons (single waves: up or hole, never two side by side), meet only the light's top j photons, and the token
should come through.

Fixed points, none searched: k = 1, 2, 3 (plates and the word table; the top table printed from the cut word table
through the 768 whole-number gains, as the machine builds it). j = full, 3, 2 at every plate and at the top read.

SESSION'S PREDICTION (from the task, uncertain):
  k = 1: judge5 top 5 kept at 55-75%, same top word 35-55% (the record's 19/30)
  k = 2: 90-97% / 75-90%
  k = 3: 98-100% / 92-99%
  light j = 3 on top of any k: a few points more lost
  stored bits a weight: about 3.4 (k=1), 6-7 (k=2), 9 (k=3); none below ternary's 1.58
  work: about 5-15% of plain at k=2, j=3
  random-digit control (same count, not the top): loses most words

THIS RUN'S OWN EXPECTATIONS, from the picture, before anything ran:
  - Top k photons in single waves leave a value off by at most a third of its last kept photon, so the error on each
    weight is about 1/3 (k=1), 1/12 (k=2), 1/48 (k=3) of the weight at worst. That error is speckle of the same kind
    as the flat, but it does not cancel as well as the grain below place 3, because it is as big as the weight's own
    third or fourth place, which is where the extra starts (docs/08: the extra reaches 5-7 diagonals down).
  - So k=1 should lose a good share of words, k=2 should hold most, k=3 nearly all. Same ranges as above.
  - Stored: k=1 carries a top place (about 2.3-2.5 bits) and a sign (1 bit): about 3.4. Each further photon about
    2.8-3 bits (where among coin-flip places, plus up or hole). k=2 about 6.2, k=3 about 9. The word table costs a
    little more per value than a plate (its places spread wider).
  - Work at the plates, counted as lit light photons times lit plate photons: plain base 2 is about 6-7 ones a light
    value times 6-7 ones a weight. k=2, j=3 is at most 2 x 3 = 6 a product, so about 10-15% of plain; the top read
    stays near its plain cost because the printed top table has many photons whatever k is.
  - Random k photons: the kept one is often a low photon, so the weight lands far below itself: most words lost even
    at k=3. Shuffled within the plate: no words beyond chance (top 5 near 0).
  - Order-free: reordering every plate's lines with the light, and relabelling the words at the top, gives identical
    piles and identical words.
