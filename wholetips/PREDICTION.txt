THIS RUN'S OWN EXPECTATIONS, from the picture, before anything ran:
  - Sizes. k = 1 stores where the top circle sits and its sign. A plate's numbers spread over about 4-5 octaves, so
    the place costs about 2.5-3 bits and the sign 1: about 3.5-4 bits a number. Each further circle sits 2 or more
    places below the one above, gap 2 half the time, 3 a quarter, ...: about 2 bits for where, 1 for up or hole. So
    k = 2 about 6.5-7, k = 3 about 9.5-10. A little above the session's numbers.
  - Words. The onlyextra run (the base-2 machine, plates and word table cut, 2026-10-08) already gave k = 1: full
    word in the top 5 83.3%, same top word 51.8%, greedy 38/480. Cutting also the position rows, biases and gains can
    only add error, so here: k = 1 about 75-83% / 45-52%, greedy 25-45 of 480. k = 2 about 95-98% / 82-90%, greedy
    150-300. k = 3 about 99% / 95-97%, greedy 330-430.
  - Where the crude grid stands. In the pile's own picture, "lower" is lower than the pile's top, not lower than
    each number's own top. A row grid cuts every circle below one line per row: the line sits in the pile's own
    places. The tips cut below a line per number, so a small number keeps circles that sit far below the row's big
    numbers -- circles the pile cannot see. So at the same stored size, I expect the crude grid (counted at its floor)
    to keep about as many words as the tips or more at 4-6 bits. At 2-3 bits I expect the tips to keep more, because
    the row's largest number sets the line so high that most of the row goes dark. This disagrees with the session's
    prediction at 4-6 bits; the run decides.
  - Error-placement control (same errors, put on other numbers of the same row): if the words follow only how much
    error each row carries, the control keeps about the same words as the version itself.
  - By part: the word table is read twice (in at the bottom, out at the top read), so its cut should cost more words
    than any one plate's, but the 48 plates together should cost more than the word table alone. Position rows and
    the small pieces (biases, gains): small at k >= 2; at k = 1 the gains (error up to a third) could cost a lot.
