THE SESSION'S RESTATEMENT (session's words, in his picture)
  The flat is in both sides, and it has to be skipped on both sides.
  - Light side: a random group of g light lines meets every up/hole sheet once a token in the group table; the light's
    ups and holes pair off inside every ready pile there, once, and every gate whose sheet matches gets that pairing.
  - Plate side: every weight is 16 places of ups and holes (w + 2^15). Above a (gate, group)'s own largest weight,
    every sheet c..14 is the sign sheet (place 15) flipped. Those sheets are flat: in the doubled pile they cancel
    against each other and against the sign sheet, all but one copy, which stands at place c:
        sum over b = c..15 of 2^b (2 bit_b - 1) = 2^c (2 bit_15 - 1)          (bit_b = 1 - bit_15 for b = c..14)
    So that gate meets that group's sign sheet ONCE, at place c, and skips sheets c+1..15 and their photons.
  - Every place of every plate is up/hole (no signed digits anywhere), so the per-place choice is retired; the run
    takes its place, decided per (gate, group) by the plate alone, before any light is seen.
  - c for a cell = the smallest c with every weight of the gate in that group in [-2^c, 2^c).
  - The identity per line: sum over b of 2^b (2 bit_b - 1) = 2w + 1, so 2 x.w = (sum of the read packets, each at its
    place) - X; X the light summed, taken once from each gate (kcorr = -1); the halving is a shift. Exact.
